Jinji Lake Waterfront

Impression of Suzhou
Individual work
Suzhou, China, 2012

At the northeast corner of Jinji Lake in the Suzhou Industrial Park, this project connects Suzhou’s 2,500-year-old identity with its modern waterfront. An “internal river” runs parallel to the lake edge as a narrative path, stringing together nodes drawn from water lanes, classical gardens, Kunqu opera, silk and Taihu stone, and linking the historic city axis to the water. The plan moves through three zones: a formal city-image section for visitors in the north, a commercial section with sunken steps and exhibition space in the centre, and an ecological section for residents in the south, where the edge softens from paved terraces to planted banks.
