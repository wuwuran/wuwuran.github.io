Redefine Buffer Zone

Ecological urbanism at the Shenzhen–Hong Kong frontier
Group work
Frontier Closed Area, 2016

The Frontier Closed Area between Shenzhen and Hong Kong is shrinking as the border softens, and the buffer it once protected is under pressure from two expanding cities. Instead of filling the gap, this proposal lets wildlife define the urban form. Continuous green belts and wetlands keep migration routes open alongside transit, existing fish ponds are kept to clean water and cool the site, and a shared “third space” serves both Hong Kong’s compact, ageing population and Shenzhen’s young, sprawling workforce. Development is phased from 2017 to 2060, growing from a low-density resource centre (FAR 0.56) to a mixed-use district (FAR 3.0) so the ecosystem can recover before the city fully arrives.
