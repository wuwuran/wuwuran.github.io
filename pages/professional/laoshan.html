Industry · Urban renewal

Laoshan Innovation Valley

Technology Finance District
Shanghai Urban Planning and Design Research Institute (SUPDRI)
Qingdao, July–October 2015
Second Prize, Shandong Province Excellent Urban and Rural Planning, 2017

Overview

An urban redevelopment in Qingdao’s Laoshan District that turns a 14.2 km² industrial corridor into a district for finance, technology and creative industries, organised as three clusters. At its centre is the Technology Finance Center, the “Financial Blue Harbor”: a waterfront financial zone along the Zhangcun River, with headquarters towers and R&D offices on the north bank, commercial and mixed-use living on the south bank, a green network linking the river to the mountains, and a 250-metre landmark tower.

My role

Lead designer for the Technology Finance District.
