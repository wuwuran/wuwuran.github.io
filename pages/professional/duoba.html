Duoba New City

Master plan and urban development project
Xining, Qinghai Province, March–July 2016

Overview

A master plan for a new city 25 km west of downtown Xining, covering a planning area of 165 km², of which about 50 km² is new construction. Duoba is positioned as the city’s western gateway and sub-centre. The plan turns the linear Huangshui River into a meandering ecological corridor and builds a garden city into mountainous terrain, with a 320-hectare culture and wetland leisure zone at its core that combines water purification, folk-culture tourism, education facilities and waterfront living.
