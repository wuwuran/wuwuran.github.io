Yangpu Waterfront Regeneration

Technological Innovation Forest
Yangpu District, Shanghai, September–December 2017

Overview

The regeneration of Yangpu’s north-central waterfront, turning an enclosed, single-use industrial corridor into an open, ecological and socially inclusive riverfront. Under the vision of a “Technological Innovation Forest”, the design treats the waterfront as a productive urban landscape where innovation industries, historic industrial structures, blue-green infrastructure and everyday public life support one another. Century-old factories such as the Shanghai Cable Plant and the Shanghai Machine Tool Plant are reused as workspaces, a shared library and exhibition halls.

My role

Spatial strategy, heritage reuse and landscape design.
